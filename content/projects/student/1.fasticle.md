## FastICLE

FastICLE implements the ICLE (In-Context Learning Experts) paradigm: a
four-stage pipeline — Dispatcher, Caster, Runtime, Assembler — that
decomposes a task into a DAG, trains and assigns specialized Expert Agents to
each step via in-context reinforcement learning, runs independent steps in
parallel, and synthesizes the results.

Experts are trained on demand with no fine-tuning or gradient updates, and
persist their refined strategies across runs. Built on `agno` for
orchestration and `fasticrl` ([the sibling project](/projects/student/fasticrl)) for
the in-context reinforcement learning. Published as the `FastICLE` package on
PyPI.
